How this works. Each starter is converted to a regressed RA/9 from FIP components (HR, BB, K) blended with ERA and pulled toward league average by sample size, then combined with that team's bullpen for the innings the starter is not projected to cover. Offenses are park-neutralized season run rates nudged by 3-week form. The two run expectations are simulated 30,000 times with a negative-binomial run distribution to produce win, run-line and total probabilities. League baseline: 4.47 R/G, 4.17 ERA.
