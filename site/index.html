How this works. Each starter is converted to a regressed RA/9 from FIP components (HR, BB, K) blended with ERA and pulled toward league average by sample size, then combined with that team's bullpen for the innings the starter is not projected to cover. Offenses are park-neutralized season run rates nudged by 3-week form. The two run expectations are simulated 30,000 times with a negative-binomial run distribution to produce win, run-line and total probabilities. League baseline: 4.48 R/G, 4.17 ERA.

Fair odds are model prices, not book prices. Where a real sportsbook line appears above, it was read from a named source. Lines are never estimated — a blank means no line was found, because a guessed line is worse than no line.

Hit rate is not profit. On the companion board's 140 priced graded picks, the ones whose model probability beat the de-vigged price went 41-45 (47.7%) for +10.4% ROI, while the ones that lost to the price went 35-19 (64.8%) for +3.9%. The worse record made more money because it was paid better. So the price is frozen here alongside each pick and the record carries ROI, not just wins.
